Augment Humankind

The problem

Public talk about AI centers on replacement. Less work shows AI used as a material for human creativity, under human direction.

What I built

Augment Humankind is a creative AI project site. It holds interactive pieces that explore human and AI creativity together, with the human as author and the AI as instrument.

Stack

TypeScript and React, built with Vite, deployed on Replit. The pieces run in the browser.

My role

I designed and directed Augment Humankind. The pieces were built with agentic coding tools under my direction.
